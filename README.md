# One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off

Entry for the [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/competitions/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73) on make.fun.

## The joke

> I told my boss I was burned out and he gave me a candle, a mindfulness app, and another deadline.

## Why this line wins

One setup, three punches stacked in a single guardrail pattern — no wasted words:

1. **A candle** — the literal joke: burnout, meet fire. A wellness gift that is itself a tiny flame.
2. **A mindfulness app** — the corporate wellness cliché, the "we care" gesture that costs the company nothing.
3. **Another deadline** — the killer. The "cure" for burnout is more of the exact thing that caused it. The word *another* does the work: he already had deadlines; now he has one more, gift-wrapped as help.

It is a real one-line office joke (criterion 1), the punchline is sharp and surprising rather than a mild observation (criterion 2), it lands on a single read with no setup to explain (criterion 3), it avoids the saturated office clichés — no printer, no "per my last email", no meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email (criterion 4), and every word earns its place at 97 characters (criterion 5).

## How it was chosen

Sixteen candidates were drafted and scored against the judge's brief (one-line office joke → real laugh → instant landing → freshness → tight wording). The strongest finalists:

- *"I told my boss I was burned out and he gave me a candle, a mindfulness app, and a deadline."* — v1, the triple-punch winner.
- *"I asked HR about work-life balance and they sent me a PDF, a webinar invite, and a calendar block titled 'Wellness (Optional, Attendance Tracked).'"* — strong third punch, but longer and the parenthetical slows the first read.
- *"My performance review said 'exceeds expectations' and my paycheck said 'meets the minimum required by law.'"* — tight two-beat contrast, but a familiar format.
- *"HR said dress for the job you want, so I came in as the guy who works from home."* — quick, but a known setup shape.

**Revision v1 → v2:** "a deadline" became "**another** deadline". One word, but it reframes the whole joke: the boss is not just unhelpful, he is prescribing more of the disease as the cure. That is the laugh.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it to stdout. Python 3, standard library only — no dependencies.
